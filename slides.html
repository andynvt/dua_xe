1 · How to play

Rules

Saigon potholesEvery lane has random potholes. Hit one and your bike stalls and your phone buzzes.
🏆Top 3 ends the raceThe first three across the line win. 60 seconds max.
🤖Don't cheatThe server only accepts ~15 taps/second. Devtools won't help.

Up to 30 racers per race; latecomers wait for the next one.

3 · Tech

Real‑time for 30 players

👆100 ms tap batchingPhones add up taps and send one message every 100 ms.
📡50 ms tickThe screen gets the full state; phones only get their own slice so the tunnel doesn't choke.
🛡️Server decidesToken bucket ~15 taps/second, malformed JSON ignored.

A room full of devs: someone will definitely send taps: 99999.
